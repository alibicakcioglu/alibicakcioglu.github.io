SYSTEM MANUAL / CYBER FLORA

Every circuit
has a breaking point.

Power a chip by hovering over it. With a keyboard, use Tab or Shift + Tab to focus a chip and supply power.

01 / INFINITE CAPACITY

Stay as long as you like. The chip keeps glowing cyan, and you stay in control.

02 / OVERLOAD TRAP

From cold, a chip takes 3 seconds to reach 100% load. Leaving preserves its current load, which then falls by about 17 percentage points per second while you are away. Returning continues from the remaining load. At 100%, your cursor and focus lock for 4 seconds; after the lock, the chip resets to zero and turns gray. Move away and return to power it again.

On touch screens, press and hold a chip. You can always press Esc to release early or use Reset system.
